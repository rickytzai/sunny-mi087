The distinction

Speech intelligence, then adaptive conversation.

AssemblyAI supplies real-time speech and turn events. Sunny uses those events to apply a bounded conversation policy—without inferring identity, personality, emotion, or psychological traits.

How Sunny works

Speech becomes presence.

AssemblyAI produces speech and turn signals. Sunny decides when to keep listening, yield playback, and pace the next response.

  1. 01Your voice

    Natural speech enters the live audio stream.

  2. 02AssemblyAI

    Real-time SpeechStarted and turn events arrive as the conversation unfolds.

  3. 03Sunny Adaptive Layer

    Bounded preferences and a deterministic fast path select the turn action.

  4. 04Playback

    The response follows the selected pause, pacing, and interruption policy.
